Before you get here

Three things to do in the next ten minutes

Call us first, then follow the step that matches your situation.

Broken tooth or crown

Save the piece

Keep any fragment of tooth, crown or veneer. Do not try to remove remaining pieces yourself. If it is bleeding, apply gauze and rinse gently with warm water.

Toothache

Ice and pain relief

Apply ice to the outside of your cheek and take over the counter pain relief as directed on the packet to reduce pain and inflammation until you arrive.

Facial swelling

Call straight away

Swelling is always an emergency. It can be an abscess, an impacted wisdom tooth, or an infection that needs treating promptly. Do not wait to see if it settles.
